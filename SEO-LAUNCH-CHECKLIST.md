# Blue Jeans Burger Bar — SEO Launch Checklist

## Confirm before publishing

- Confirm the final domain and replace `https://bluejeansburgerbar.pt/` everywhere if needed.
- Confirm door number, postal code, phone, regular hours and holiday hours with Blue Jeans.
- Replace preview menu/prices with the current approved dine-in menu.
- Confirm permission to publish every supplied photograph.
- Ask the owners to approve the story text and Portuguese/English translations.
- Test every page at desktop, tablet and mobile widths.
- Test the mobile menu, PT/EN switch, filters, lightbox, phone, Instagram, Uber Eats and directions.
- Validate Restaurant structured data with Google Rich Results Test or Schema Markup Validator.

## Hosting and technical SEO

1. Connect the final domain and enable HTTPS.
2. Redirect HTTP to HTTPS and select one canonical hostname.
3. Configure the host to serve `404.html` for missing pages.
4. Apply `_headers` or equivalent security headers.
5. Confirm `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` load publicly.
6. Confirm every canonical and hreflang URL uses the final domain.
7. Re-run a broken-link and image-path check after deployment.

## Google, Bing and local presence

1. Add the domain to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit the sitemap.
2. Keep the business name, exact address and phone identical across the site, Google, Instagram, Tripadvisor, Uber Eats, Facebook and directories.
3. Add the website and phone to the Google Business Profile.
4. Add the approved menu, current photos and normal/holiday hours to the profile.
5. Correct older listings showing Rua Costa Pinto 100 if the owners confirm 104.
6. Encourage honest customer reviews and respond naturally; never buy reviews.

## Measurement and maintenance

- Add privacy-conscious analytics only with the required consent setup.
- Track calls, map opens, Instagram and delivery clicks as conversions.
- Review Search Console monthly for local searches such as “hambúrguer Paço de Arcos” and “burger Oeiras”.
- Update menu, hours, structured data and photography whenever they change.
- Keep Portuguese and English pages synchronized.

Submitting a sitemap helps discovery; it does not guarantee indexing or rankings.
