Born from blue

A blue wall. A young idea. A name you cannot forget.

The name came from the colour of the room. The rest found its personality at the table.

Blue Jeans opened on Rua Costa Pinto as a younger, more relaxed concept. Denim moved from the walls to the menu names, the attitude and the welcome.
