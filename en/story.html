Our story

Bornblue.

The name was on the walls

A colour became an attitude.

Fernando Matos and Marina Alonso looked at the space on Rua Costa Pinto. The inspiration was right there in front of them: the blue walls.

Owners of nearby Casa Galega, they imagined a different, younger concept. Blue Jeans was born as a naturally informal burger bar, with denim woven into its identity.

The name was not borrowed from a trend. It came from the place itself. The denim world then moved into the menu, the décor and the venue’s personality.

Today, the experience brings together burgers named Blue Jeans, Denim and 501, starters to share, vegetarian choices and drinks for a table in no hurry.
